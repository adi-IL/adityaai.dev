Scrollsilk Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026.

Scrollsilk is an Android teleprompter, script reader, and local camera recorder published by the developer named on its Google Play listing. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It applies to Scrollsilk 2.0.0 and later releases until a new policy replaces it.

The short version

  • Scrollsilk collects no personal data.
  • It makes no network requests and has no internet permission.
  • Camera and microphone access are optional and used only on your device when you open the in-app camera preview or record a take.
  • It has no account, ads, analytics, or crash reporting.
  • Scripts and recorded takes stay in private storage on your device until you share or delete them, and uninstalling the app removes them.

What the app does

Scrollsilk turns a phone or tablet into a teleprompter. You can type or paste a script, import a TXT, Markdown, DOCX, or text-based PDF file, read it in a full-screen reader with auto-scroll and horizontal mirror, and optionally open a movable camera preview window inside the reader to record MP4 video and audio takes (1080p target with device-dependent quality fallback) directly on your device. Scrollsilk does not float over other camera apps and does not record in the background when you leave the app.

Data we collect

None.

Scrollsilk does not collect, transmit, sell, or automatically share personal data with the developer or any third party. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, account, or payment details. It does not access your contacts, location, or background media library.

The app has no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting upload. It does not read or use an advertising ID. It does not create a profile of you, your scripts, or your recordings.

Permissions

Scrollsilk asks for runtime permissions only when you choose to use the feature that needs them:

  • Camera (CAMERA): requested only when you tap to open the in-app camera preview or start recording. Video frames stay on your device for the live preview or the local MP4 recording you started.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): requested only when you tap Record to capture audio into your local MP4 take. Scrollsilk does not listen in the background or perform voice tracking.

If you use Scrollsilk solely as a display-only reader beside an external camera or teleprompter rig, you never have to grant camera or microphone permissions. Scrollsilk does not request location, contacts, notifications, draw-over-other-apps, or broad storage permissions.

When you import a file, the Android system picker or share sheet gives Scrollsilk temporary access to that one file. The app reads the file, copies the text into its own private storage, and stops using the original file. It does not scan, list, or open other files.

When you tap Share on a saved recording, Scrollsilk grants temporary single-file access to that one MP4 via Android's FileProvider so the app you pick in the Android share sheet can receive it.

Keeping the screen on while you read or record uses a window setting, not a permission.

Network access

Scrollsilk has no internet permission in its release build and makes no network requests. Import, library, settings, reading, camera preview, recording, and playback work with Wi-Fi and mobile data disabled.

No script text, audio, video, usage event, device identifier, or diagnostic data is sent anywhere by the app.

Local storage

Scripts, reading settings, drafts, and recorded MP4 takes are stored in app-private storage on your device (filesDir/recordings for takes and appDataDir for scripts and settings). Android keeps this storage private from other apps and does not add recordings to your public photo gallery automatically. Scrollsilk does not add encryption of its own to stored scripts or recordings and does not claim to be a secure vault.

The app has no cloud sync and no backup service. It does not copy your scripts or recordings to a server, another device, or a cloud account. Android system backup is disabled for Scrollsilk, so your scripts and recordings are not copied to your Google account or transferred to another device by the operating system.

DOCX and PDF conversion and video recording run entirely on the device.

Deleting your data

You can delete any script or any recorded take inside the app. Deleting a recording immediately removes both its metadata and its private MP4 file. Uninstalling Scrollsilk or clearing its app data removes all local scripts, drafts, settings, and recordings from that device. Scrollsilk keeps no copy elsewhere.

Because the app collects no personal data, there is no account or profile to access, correct, export, or delete on our side.

Third parties

Scrollsilk includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and it shares no personal data or recordings automatically with third parties.

If you explicitly share a recording using Android's share sheet or open it in an external video player, the destination app you choose receives that file under its own terms.

Google Play distributes the app and processes the purchase. Google handles the payment and may collect information about the transaction under Google's privacy policy. The app itself does not receive your payment details or Google account information.

Children

Scrollsilk is a tool for creators and presenters and is not directed to children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app or its data practices change, this policy is updated and the effective date at the top changes. A change that affects data handling is described here before the release that makes it.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the Scrollsilk listing on Google Play. The listing is the current source for the support address.
